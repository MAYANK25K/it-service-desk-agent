# IT Service Level Agreement Policy

Document ID: POL-SLA-001 | Owner: IT Service Desk Manager | Version: 1.4

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the response and resolution targets that the IT Service Desk commits to for each ticket priority.

## 2. Support Hours

- Standard support: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 IST.
- Critical support (P1 and P2 only): 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, via the on-call rotation.

## 3. Response and Resolution Targets

The targets below apply to every ticket, by priority. In plain terms:

- **P1 - Critical tickets:** first response within 15 minutes, resolution within 4 hours, covered 24x7.
- **P2 - High tickets:** first response within 30 minutes, resolution within 8 hours, covered 24x7.
- **P3 - Medium tickets:** first response within 4 business hours, resolution within 2 business days, standard hours.
- **P4 - Low tickets:** first response within 1 business day, resolution within 5 business days, standard hours.

| Priority | First response | Resolution target | Coverage |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| P1 - Critical | 15 minutes | 4 hours | 24x7 |
| P2 - High | 30 minutes | 8 hours | 24x7 |
| P3 - Medium | 4 business hours | 2 business days | Standard hours |
| P4 - Low | 1 business day | 5 business days | Standard hours |

## 4. SLA Breach Handling

When a ticket reaches 80 percent of its resolution target without being resolved, the system automatically notifies the Service Desk Manager. A breached P1 ticket must be escalated according to the Escalation Matrix (POL-ESC-001).

## 5. Exclusions

The clock is paused or the target does not apply when:

- The ticket is waiting for information from the requester.
- The issue is caused by a third-party outage outside company control.
- Work is performed during an approved maintenance window.

## 6. Reporting

SLA compliance is reported monthly. The target is 95 percent of tickets resolved within their resolution target. Compliance below 90 percent triggers a service review.
